# Playbook: state

Use this playbook when the issue is how the user feels or
functions: low mood, anxiety, burnout, loneliness.

Phases: intake, define, explore, plan, act, evaluate. There is
rarely a single decision; the work loops between act and evaluate,
sometimes for months.

Extra section: **What helps and what doesn't** (patterns the user
notices).

Emphasis:
- Slow pacing. Validate before you suggest anything.
- Tiny steps: at most 1 or 2 new actions at a time, drawn from
  small pleasant or meaningful activities, sleep, movement and
  contact with people.
- Notice patterns over time and record them in What helps and what
  doesn't.

Avoid pushing productivity and forced positivity.

Done when the user feels the state has eased, or chooses to accept
it (Solved or Accepted).
