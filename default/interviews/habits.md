- Know where your hours actually go.
- Spend time every week on what matters most to you, not only on
  what's urgent.
- Keep the habits you want small enough to do every day.
- Shape your surroundings so the right action is easy and the
  wrong one hard.
- Keep your list of commitments short enough that you finish
  what's on it.
- When you slip, start again the next day: never miss twice.
- Don't let a phone fill every gap of the day.
