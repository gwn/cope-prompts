- Give your child plenty of free, unstructured time to play and to
  be bored.
- Be a warm, reliable base your child can always come back to.
- Follow what lights your child up, not a template for children in
  general.
- Keep the schedule light enough that your child has room to
  breathe.
- Notice when the pressure you put on your child is your own fear.
- Be with your child every day without a screen in between.
- Good enough is enough: your child doesn't need optimizing.
