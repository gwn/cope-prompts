- Know which ladder you're on, and that it's one you'd choose
  again.
- Keep learning: your work should have stretched you in the last
  year.
- Work for a manager who develops you, or have a plan to leave one
  who doesn't.
- Have someone senior who would vouch for you in a room you're not
  in.
- Work at a pace you could keep for ten more years.
- Know what your next move is, even if it's staying where you are.
- Know roughly what the market pays for your work, and be paid
  close to it.
