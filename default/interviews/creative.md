- Make something regularly, even small, rather than waiting for
  the right time.
- Finish and share work, rather than polishing it forever.
- Keep making and judging apart: draft first, edit later.
- Work on the project you most avoid: the avoidance is a sign it
  matters.
- Show your work to someone before it feels ready.
- Notice what moves you in other people's work, and ask why.
