- Make the big decisions of your life yourself, not to meet your
  family's expectations.
- Be able to say no to your parents without days of guilt.
- See your family as often as you choose, not as often as you're
  made to.
- Know which roles your family gave you, and whether you still
  want them.
- Keep in touch out of choice, not duty alone: examine what you're
  told you owe.
- Put your own household before the family you grew up in.
