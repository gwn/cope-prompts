- Sleep seven to nine hours, at roughly the same times every day.
- Get daylight early in the day, and keep the evenings dim.
- Move your body most days, and get out of breath a few times a
  week.
- Eat mostly real food: junk and ultra-processed food is the
  exception, not the base.
- Keep alcohol, nicotine and other substances occasional, never
  what gets you through the day.
- Keep screens and feeds out of bed, and out of the first and last
  hour of the day.
- Your energy should hold through the day without being propped up
  by caffeine and sugar.
- Do something hard on purpose now and then: cold, real effort, an
  early start.
