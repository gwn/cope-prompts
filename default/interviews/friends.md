- Have a few close friends you could call in a crisis.
- See friends in person regularly, on purpose, rather than leaving
  it to chance.
- Have a standing time with someone: a weekly walk, a monthly
  dinner.
- Reach out first, rather than only waiting to be asked.
- Belong to something beyond work and family: a club, a team, a
  group, a place.
- Keep a wider ring of looser ties, not only the closest few.
