- Be able to name what you feel, not only that you're "fine" or
  "stressed".
- Talk to yourself the way you'd talk to a friend, not with
  contempt.
- Have someone safe you can tell the things you're ashamed of.
- Turn toward old pain rather than keeping busy to avoid it.
- Notice what your body is telling you: tension, tiredness, a
  knot.
- Rest without having to earn it first.
- Say no without needing everyone to be pleased with you.
