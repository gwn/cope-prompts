- Spend less than you earn, every month.
- Know where your money goes, at least roughly.
- Keep a few months of expenses set aside for emergencies.
- Carry no high-interest debt from month to month.
- Save automatically, on the day your pay arrives, before you can
  spend it.
- Insure against what would ruin you.
- Spend freely on what you value, and cut what you don't care
  about.
- Be able to look at your accounts without dread.
