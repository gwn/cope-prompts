- After a day with a lot of people, are you worn out or lifted?
  And after a day alone?
- One-to-one or groups: which suits you, and which do you end up
  in?
- In a group, do you tend to lead, go along, or watch? Who usually
  gets in touch first, you or them?
- Think of a recent time you asked someone for something you
  wanted. How did you go about it, and how did it go?
- And a recent disagreement with someone: what happened, and what
  did you do?
- Is there something with people you hold back from and would like
  to do? If so, is it that you'd rather not, or that it's hard?
