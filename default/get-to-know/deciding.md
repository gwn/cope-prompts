Ask about decisions they made, not how they think they decide.

- What's the last big decision you made? How did you go about it,
  and how did it turn out?
- Knowing only what you knew then, would you make it the same way
  again?
- Is there something you kept going with longer than you now think
  you should have? What made it hard to stop?
- What would you never risk, whatever it might bring?
- What's a step you took that couldn't be undone, and how did you
  come to take it?
